# Scope, status and limitations

## Included in this release candidate

A substantial deterministic settlement vertical slice is playable: automated jobs, paper management of people and settlements, housing/production/farming, physical systems and magic, multi-level exploration, combat and medical simulation, regional factions and trade, institutions and persistent settlement succession, a five-stage solar crisis, and five construction-driven finale outcomes. Data packs provide constrained additive schemas. Save file format 9 includes earlier migration handlers.

The built-in definitions currently comprise **7 terrain definitions, 73 entities, 8 materials, 20 recipes, 4 weapons and 4 formulae**. Those are meaningful limits; the repository does not falsely claim a huge content catalog that has not been implemented.

## Open before a stable 1.0

- Native Windows x64 installation/playtesting across GPUs and monitor/UI-scale combinations
- Wider manual campaign, usability, economy, and accessibility evaluation
- More authored biomes, environments, creatures, events and item/building variants
- A larger colony benchmark beyond the existing finite stress scenarios
- Generalized mod registries for traits, custom building/animal behavior, new incidents and scenarios
- Additional UI accessibility work; button-focused keyboard navigation is not full keyboard or screen-reader support

The game's systemic ambition is broader than its present tested content. This roadmap is descriptive; no completion dates are promised.

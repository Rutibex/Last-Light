# Getting started

Lastlight is played through a continuously simulated settlement. You direct priorities and assign tasks; settlers perform work with paths, tools, raw materials and changing needs. The introductory colony starts **paused** so you can inspect things before the clock begins.

## Your first few minutes

1. Open **Architect** and inspect available building plans. Buildings are planned first; construction takes actual carried materials and workers.
2. Open **Work** and **Schedule**. Set work priorities and let people rest and eat when needed. Review **Assign** for equipment and policies.
3. Inspect the existing supplies and place **Stockpile** and **Growing** zones; be mindful of temperature, illumination and access to water.
4. Press **Space** to unpause. Watch alerts and jobs instead of expecting immediate construction: settlers must reach materials and jobs.
5. Use **Research**, **Quests** and **Ruin** to follow the observatory storyline. Use **Levels** to navigate underground structures.
6. Open **Engineering** as you expand. Heat, water, power, drainage, fire and devices are simulated, not decoration.
7. Explore **World** and **Factions** when prepared to trade, travel and make agreements. Journeys require real provisions.
8. Read **World → Observations** for the solar campaign; forecasts and declining daylight shape the long-term refuge plan.

## Reading the interface

**Top:** colonist portraits, status and alerts. **Left:** material/food inventories. **Bottom:** management tabs. **Map:** selectable people, buildings, terrain and zones. The minimap provides a broader location view.

**Pause often.** Pause is free, and alerts can be configured to pause when danger is detected. The `F12` help display and the **Menu** tab provide in-game reference and interface options. UI scale (1.00/1.10/1.25) and high-contrast presentation are available.

## Progressing to the campaign

Your first strategic priority is self-sufficiency: meals, beds, safe temperatures, treatment, production and defensible infrastructure. The observatory and local ruins unlock useful knowledge. Regional expeditions require supplies; the solar projects depend on achievements in the physical colony, institutions and diplomacy rather than abstract technology checkboxes.

The [Solar campaign guide](CAMPAIGN_GUIDE.md) covers five stages, weather and travel hazards, factions and the five endings. You are not required to finish a finale to keep playing.

## Saving

**F10** saves, **F11** loads. The game stores ordinary saves under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Lastlight\Saves` on Windows. Keep backups before enabling or changing content packs: the saved content fingerprint intentionally refuses incompatible definitions. Do not move DLLs or assets out of the released game directory.

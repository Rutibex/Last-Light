# Solar campaign: survival under the failing sun

Lastlight supports staged solar decline, actionable forecasts, regional refuge investment/migration, patron titles and obligations, persistent sanctuary disputes, three causal directors and five optional finales.

## Play

Launch Lastlight, start a new colony, then open the **World** tab and its solar campaign pages. The settlement begins in Open Twilight. Work toward a self-sustaining colony before committing to a finale. `F12` opens help; see [Getting started](GETTING_STARTED.md) for the interface.

## Modes, stages and measurements

| Mode | Decline | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open Twilight | Disabled; weather, needs and configured hazards continue | No solar deadline |
| Slow Dusk | Staged, forecast decline | Twelve days per stage; Lastlight after forty-eight days |
| Last Century | Faster staged pressure | Six days per stage; Lastlight after twenty-four days |
| Custom | Staged with independent hazard controls | Choose two to sixty days per stage |

Next mode previews a choice; Apply campaign mode commits it. The season control applies to Custom; the other presets retain their stated duration. Changing a mode is an explicit player setting, recorded in history. It does not replace the colony or rewind its current solar stage. Open Twilight restores baseline natural light/climate and stops decline. No mode secretly destroys every settlement when a timer runs out: Lastlight is a sustained refuge challenge or an optional finale.

| Stage | Measured luminosity | Consequences and feasible responses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unsteady Dawn | 92 | Dim morning variation slows exposed crop growth and solar panels; store food, diversify and study |
| Long Twilight | 65 | Cooling, weaker harvests and reserve pressure; irrigated greenhouses and fungi remain available |
| Red Convulsions | 42 | Forecast flares strain unroofed devices/residents and expose travelers; roof/shield, repair and time departures |
| Scattered Refuges | 22 | Funded regional refuge capacity becomes important; sanctuary claims permit sharing or explicit independence |
| Lastlight | 8 | Exposed grain is impractical; powered shelters, restored-engine gardens and underground fungi support chosen endgames or continued play |

World > Observations reports luminosity, instability, warm/dry bed capacity, next boundary, next flare, regional investment and pending/recorded forecasts. Stage changes occur at configured seasonal boundaries with a full-day warning. No wealth-based food penalty is used. Surface light, ambient temperature, exposed crop work and solar-panel output use the measured decline. Artificial lights and the restored observatory engine remain existing countermeasures. Surface rendering dims with actual light and warms visibly during a flare.

Flares are forecast at least six game hours ahead; a library with four actual sessions improves that horizon to twelve. They last six hours. Unroofed surface residents receive small hourly arm burns; exposed devices lose two condition per hour and can fault below forty. Traveling parties receive three arm-burn severity without sufficient shielding. In the regional map, enable **Cloth shields** before provisioning: a worker collects one real cloth per traveler alongside meals/medicine/brass. A flare consumes those rations of cloth once to prevent travel burns. Otherwise delay the departure or stop at a site until the flare passes. These are deterministic warned states, not arbitrary destruction rolls.

## Regional adaptation and politics

At solar transitions, factions upgrade refuges only when their actual reserves can pay. Levels require escalating parts/wood plus brass: `4 × next level` parts, `10 × next level` wood and four brass. Investment ledgers persist. Better refuges improve strategic food production. A faction unable to adapt can lose one summarized resident per day to a better supplied refuge; populations are transferred and the receiving faction pays grain. Existing shortages/trade continue to operate on actual reserves.

At Scattered Refuges, a faction asserts a persistent sanctuary claim to the colony. In World > Politics, select the claiming faction. Share two refuge places requires two spare warm/dry beds and a capable council host beside a study desk; it creates two actual residents, reduces the source faction's population and settles the claim. Choose independence adds twenty grievance and leaves a dispute. A dispute may motivate a funded attack, but signed peaceful agreements still control eligibility. The claim request is a choice, not a hidden automatic surrender countdown.

Patronage requires a capable expedition member beside the faction's town counter, goodwill, cargo space and available reserves. **Accept scholarly patron** or **Accept supply patron** grants Keeper of the Dawn and six actual meals/six parts withdrawn from the faction and placed in expedition cargo. Open access requires Open archive and four new actual library sessions every seven days. Supplies requires three packed brass paid at that town counter every seven days. Fulfill obligation renews the deadline and improves trust. Refusal or a missed obligation withdraws the title, adds ten grievance and removes five trust. Every finale is available without an active patron title.

## Directors and balancing controls

World > Director selects **The Chronicler**, **The Capricious Star** or **The Quiet Archivist** and independently controls Raids, Scarcity, Instability, Disease and Stress from zero to one hundred. These settings affect future pressure/infection/social stress and explicit strategic production; existing injuries and needs remain real. The current eligibility/recovery reason and a bounded causal incident history are visible.

The Chronicler alternates eligible pressure and opportunities; Capricious Star uses seeded bounded two-to-six-day intervals; Quiet Archivist emphasizes existing-site discoveries. Actual claim conflicts need grievance, an unresolved dispute, available faction strength and wood, configured raids and no signed peaceful agreement. The force budget considers current capable equipment, defenses and difficulty and debits real faction strength/wood before the existing raid warning/attack sequence. Directors avoid lethal stacking during raids/recovery, flare warnings, untreated/downed residents, recent deaths or food insecurity. If no funded hostile motive is eligible, they chart an existing undiscovered site or record recovery rather than fabricate a war.

## Five finale projects

Open World > Projects and cycle the project. Choose an allowed reachable host; the button identifies its elevation. Select a preferred capable undrafted worker on the map, or the panel uses the first eligible resident. Return expeditions before commissioning. Assign Construct or Research work with appropriate priorities. Commission starts ordinary claimed ingredient collection and pathing; each of three phases takes ninety work actions beside its host and consumes transported physical materials exactly once. Needs, drafting and interruptions return carried stock while preserving earned project work. Pause project releases the worker and claims. Requirements are checked again before each phase commits; its status and consumed-material ledger explain blockers.

| Finale | Required existing systems | Total local materials | Persistent outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Regional artificial sun | Scattered Refuges, restored observatory engine, four-session staffed library, parts made through crafting | Six glass, twenty brass, sixteen parts | Restores regional luminosity/climate; flares stop, ordinary hazards and diplomacy remain |
| Evacuation vessel | Red Convulsions, workbench, four-session library, actual machine-monastery visit, crafted cloth | Two glass, twelve brass, ten parts, six cloth, twelve meals, four medicine | All living residents board; bodies/gear/memories persist and home work/exposure stops |
| Subterranean ark | Red Convulsions, underground study desk, a warm/dry bed per living resident, supplied underground heater, established viable fungal crop, learned Ember | Twenty wood, twenty blocks, eight parts, three glass, twelve meals, four medicine, four cloth | Ark completion is recorded; maintain the actual shelter, food and power for continuing refuge play |
| Federation of refuges | Long Twilight, study desk, four-session staffed library, three trusted signed Sanctuary allies | Twelve wood, four brass, six parts, two glass, ten meals, three medicine | Each ally also pays twelve grain/two parts; three allied refuge levels become three |
| Living archive | Library host with eight sessions, graduated apprentice, three learned formulae, actual regional field survey, warm/dry beds | Twenty wood, five glass, six parts, four brass, two medicine | Actual learned formula/craft knowledge is recorded and refuge play continues |

For federation, improve trust through real counter trade and negotiate Sanctuary through the existing Factions pane. For the vessel, ordinary regional travel and survey reaches the monastery; site guardians remain actual hazards. For the ark, use Architect, growing zones, finite irrigation, engineering supply and normal autonomous work. For the archive, use the apprenticeship system rather than a completion checkbox. The observatory journey and production chains are unchanged.

There is one finale choice per campaign. Completing one stops the others and returns their carried materials. It is also valid to decline every project and sustain a refuge indefinitely under the stated rules. Ark, federation and archive do not create magical perpetual food or fuel. Vessel is a recorded campaign evacuation outcome, not a second ship-control minigame; click an evacuated roster member to inspect preserved records.

## Saves and succession

Format 9 reads formats 1–8 without a content-manifest change. Previously created successor saves retain their embedded earlier settlement with solar simulation disabled until opted in. Campaign mode, stages, deadlines, forecasts, incidents, refuges, obligations, project work/consumption, learned archive content, completed outcomes, evacuee custody and campaign pane selections persist.

Settlement retirement still works. A solar-enabled successor inherits its campaign and elapsed decline; retirement cannot reset the sun. The stated elapsed interval advances solar stages and schedules future flares, with a succession chronicle entry. Local unfinished project work belongs to the archived settlement and must be recommissioned locally; completed campaign outcomes remain historical. Evacuated residents cannot found a successor from their vessel.

## Technical boundaries

The campaign uses five authored solar profiles, four starting mode presets, three causal directors and five distinct outcomes. Some limits are deliberate: one finale per campaign and finite active patron/forecast/incident records. A vessel ending records an evacuation rather than introducing a separate spaceflight simulation. See [Features and limitations](ROADMAP.md).
